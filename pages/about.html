ABOUT THE LAB

Learn structure before decoration.

A practical collection for understanding meaningful, maintainable web documents.

Our approach

HTML gives content its structure and meaning. This repository focuses on semantic elements, useful metadata, accessible controls and predictable navigation.

Who is it for?

  • Beginners learning HTML5.
  • Students who need examples to practise.
  • Developers who want reusable starting points.
  • Contributors improving document quality.

Project principles

Examples should be readable, local-first and honest about their limitations. A client-side form is never described as sending data.
